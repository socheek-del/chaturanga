# Apocalypse rules, adoption and rights

**Research date:** 2026-11-26  
**Decision:** Block implementation pending a digital-adaptation license or written permission. No contact has been made.

## Selected edition and evidence

Use C. S. Elliott's 1976 two-player edition on a 5×5 board. Each side has two Horsemen that move as chess
knights and five Footmen that move as chess pawns without an opening double-step. Both players secretly
select one move and reveal it together. When both target one square, a Horseman removes a Footman; matching
piece types remove both. A Footman whose intended capture target moved still makes the diagonal step. Illegal
declared moves cost a penalty point; two penalties lose. A Footman reaching the far rank promotes to a
Horseman if its side has fewer than two; otherwise it is redeployed to a vacant square off the far rank.
The game ends when one side has Footmen and the other does not, two final Footmen disappear together, two
penalties accrue, or no legal move remains while Footmen survive (draw).

The precise initial formation should be rechecked against an original rules diagram before an engine
fixture is committed. The accessible summaries identify the game and its standard inventory, but their
diagram alt text is not a substitute for that check. Wikipedia cites *Games & Puzzles* issue 53; another
catalog summary says issue 53 as well, but the primary issue was not available in this review.

No comparable participation figures establish a most-played edition. This is the documented catalog target,
not a popularity ranking.

## Rights review

Ludii and the rules summaries identify C. S. Elliott and date the game to 1976. The materials reviewed do
not establish a present rights holder or provide a digital-adaptation license for this project. Under the
repository's gate for modern authored titles, do not proceed to a faithful digital product until applicable
permission or a license is documented. This is a project condition, not a jurisdiction-wide legal conclusion.
No contact was made, and no third-party rules prose or artwork has been copied into the product.
