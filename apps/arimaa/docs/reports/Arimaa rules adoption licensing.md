# Choose Standard Arimaa, Clear Its License

Select the **official standard two-player Arimaa rules** for the product; do not substitute Fast Rules or a theme variant. The official rules define a complete, sourceable game, and the human World Championship has published results through 2026, but the evidence does not establish that any variant is empirically most popular. Arimaa also has a versioned public license that covers a free public game site, but Section 3 requires a community announcement and specific notices. **No Discord announcement or rights-holder contact has been made**, so public implementation must wait until the project can satisfy that condition; any ad, fee, sponsorship, or other revenue instead requires Section 2’s commercial agreement.

## The official 8×8 rules give a complete product core

The standard game uses an **8×8 board**, four trap squares at c3, f3, c6, and f6, and two 16-piece armies: an Elephant, Camel, two Horses, two Dogs, two Cats, and eight Rabbits. Gold places its army on its two home ranks first, Silver then places its army on its own two home ranks, and Gold moves first. Setups are freely chosen within those ranks rather than copied from a canonical opening. ([Official game rules](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/rulesIntro.html), [official notation](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/notation.html), [2024 rules analysis](https://research-portal.uu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/240416931/LIPIcs.FUN.2024.27.pdf))

Each turn uses up to **four orthogonal one-square steps**. A Rabbit cannot move backward under its own power. A strictly stronger adjacent piece can push or pull one weaker enemy piece through a two-step sequence, completed atomically in the same turn. A piece is frozen by an adjacent stronger enemy unless it has a friendly orthogonal neighbor; an unsupported piece on a trap is immediately captured. A completed turn must change the position. These mechanics call for a turn editor that can show intermediate choices while checking legality and trap consequences against the final turn state. ([Official game rules](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/rulesIntro.html), [2024 rules analysis](https://research-portal.uu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/240416931/LIPIcs.FUN.2024.27.pdf))

A player wins by reaching the far rank with a Rabbit at turn end, capturing all opposing Rabbits, or leaving the opponent without a legal move. The official rules forbid a third repetition of the same position with the same player to move; if every legal-looking turn would create the prohibited repetition, that player loses. The rules specify a result-check order for simultaneous goal, elimination, immobilization, and repetition events, so the engine should implement that order exactly. ([Official game rules](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/rulesIntro.html), [official notation](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/notation.html))

## Adoption supports the standard profile, not a popularity claim

The official site separates **Official Rules** from **Fast Rules**, which it presents as optional faster or partly luck-based variations, and from football/rugby-themed explanations. That status makes standard Arimaa the defensible selection; the source does not compare audiences among variants. ([Official Learn to Play Arimaa](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/), [Fast Rules PDF](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/homeRules.pdf), [Football/Rugby theme rules](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/learn/FootballTheme.pdf))

Historical activity evidence shows a real online community but cannot quantify its current size. A thesis reports downloading **261,080 archived games through March 23, 2013**; those are games, not unique players. A 2015 article estimated “perhaps around 500 active players,” explicitly as a qualified estimate now over a decade old. Official human championship results continue through 2026, but the results page lists no entrant counts and its computer-championship summary stops at 2015. None of these sources measures 2026 unique players or compares standard and Fast Rules use. ([McKee thesis](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/papers/PatrickMcKee/mckeep-t.pdf), [Lewis, 2015](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/papers/150301ICGA/ICGA38-1.pdf), [official championship history](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/wc/))

## Section 3 covers a free site with mandatory notices

The current Arimaa Public License, version **2025.02**, explicitly places publicly distributed software and a free online game with no user or advertising revenue under its non-commercial Section 3 route. That section requires a product announcement in the Arimaa Discord. The license does not say whether that announcement must precede development, beta, or production, so treat public release as gated and clarify timing if it affects the schedule. ([Arimaa Public License, version 2025.02](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/license/current.txt))

Section 3 also requires the product to state that it is authorized by Arimaa.com and complies with Section 3, state that derivatives must also comply with the Arimaa Public License, clearly display `www.arimaa.com`, link online offerings to `http://arimaa.com/arimaa`, and explain that end-user rights apply only to the product or service and not to Arimaa itself. The project must also keep the service free of user and advertising revenue. Ads, sponsorships, fees, or other revenue move it to Section 2, which requires a commercial agreement and at least a 1% revenue share. ([Arimaa Public License, §§2–3](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/license/current.txt))

## Keep game rights separate from code and artwork

The license grants the game, rules, name, board design, and game implementation for Section 3 use, but does not expressly list piece design in that section. Use original piece art and original tutorial prose; do not assume the game license clears every official asset. The official download page says software is under OSL 2.1 unless a file states otherwise, while the Akimot engine is GPL-3.0. Those code licenses are separate from the Arimaa game license, and adopting any engine code would add its own obligations. ([Arimaa Public License](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/license/current.txt), [official software downloads](https://arimaa.com/arimaa/download/), [Akimot repository](https://github.com/tomik/akimot))

## Conclusion

The rules profile is ready to document and implement once the permission path is made actionable. The next concrete step is to prepare the required Section 3 announcement and product notices for owner review, confirm the product will remain non-commercial, and obtain explicit authorization before anyone posts to Discord. If the project cannot accept those obligations—or later adds revenue—keep Arimaa blocked until the appropriate license is secured.
