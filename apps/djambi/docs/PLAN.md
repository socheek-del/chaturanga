# Djambi plan

## Status

Blocked before rules-engine work while digital-adaptation rights remain unverified. No publisher contact has been made.

## Selected edition

Use the standard four-player edition on a square 9×9 board, with four corner armies and the marked central Labyrinth. The roadmap chooses this established form; available sources do not compare participation across the two-player and hex-board editions, so no popularity winner is claimed. Record any rule choices against the sources before implementation if the rights gate clears.

## Rights gate

The source review identifies Jean Anesto as author and L'Impensé radical as publisher of the 1975 game. Board Game Arena's product page credits L'Impensé radical for making its digital adaptation possible. No public digital-adaptation license for this project was found. Under the repository's product gate, do not implement until an applicable license or written permission is documented. Do not contact the publisher without owner authorization. Use only original product art and tutorial wording if cleared.
