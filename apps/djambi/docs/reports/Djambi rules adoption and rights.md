# Djambi rules adoption and rights

**Research date:** 2026-11-26  
**Decision:** Block implementation pending a digital-adaptation license or written permission. No contact has been made.

## Selected planning edition

Use the standard four-seat square-board game. The catalog's linked description and Board Game Arena document a 9×9 board, the central Labyrinth, four 3×3 corner formations, and nine pieces per side: Chief, Assassin, Reporter, Necromobile, Diplomat/Provocateur, and four Militants. The other editions described by the rules site include a two-player game and hex-board games for other player counts. The available material supports four-player Djambi as the established edition, but it does not provide comparative participation data sufficient to call it the most popular form.

The main rules features requiring a future engine contract include blocked queen-like paths, Militants limited to two squares, bodies remaining as obstacles, special corpse placement on kills, the Reporter’s adjacent capture, the Diplomat’s relocation of living opponents, the Necromobile’s relocation of corpses, leadership and turn bonuses in the Labyrinth, and elimination/transfer of a Chief’s remaining forces. These are source summaries, not implementation text.

## Adoption evidence and limits

Board Game Arena lists the four-player game, credits Jean Anesto as author, L'Impensé radical as publisher, publication year 1975, and an online release in 2017. Its play counter is a time-sensitive snapshot, not evidence that this form outranks the other editions by popularity. The French rules site describes the 9×9 game and distinguishes the alternative player-count/board forms. The repository roadmap's four-player default remains a selection for a consistent product scope, not a statistical claim.

## Rights review

The available publisher/adaptation evidence points to a specific rights holder: Board Game Arena's Djambi page thanks L'Impensé radical for enabling its adaptation. The official page does not offer a general license for independent digital adaptations. A separate English rulebook published by Red Boar Studios carries its own 2019 copyright notice; that notice is specific to that edition's text and is not evidence that Red Boar owns the original game's rights.

No applicable public license for this project was found in the reviewed sources. The product remains blocked under the repository rule for authored/published games until permission or an applicable license is documented. This is a project release gate, not a jurisdiction-wide legal conclusion. No external contact was made, and the user has not authorized one. Do not reuse the publisher's or existing digital adaptation's art, text, diagrams, or identity.
