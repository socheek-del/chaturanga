# Djambi adoption notes

- Board Game Arena lists Djambi as a four-player game, attributes it to Jean Anesto, lists L'Impensé radical as publisher and 1975 as publication year, and reports online availability from 2017.
- The French rules reference describes the square 9×9 four-player setup and also mentions variants for two, three, five, and six players, with hex-board variants for larger/nonstandard counts.
- Neither source supplies comparative participation rates across editions. Use the roadmap's four-seat square edition as a planning default without claiming it is statistically the most popular.
- Sources: <https://fr.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=djambi>, <https://www.regledujeu.fr/djambi/>.
