# Djambi rights-source notes

- Board Game Arena identifies L'Impensé radical as publisher and thanks it for making the site's Djambi adaptation possible: <https://fr.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=djambi>.
- No general public license for independent digital adaptations was found on the sources reviewed.
- Red Boar Studios' 2019 English PDF states that its edition of the rules is copyrighted by Red Boar Studios: <https://redboarstudios.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Djambi-Rules.pdf>. Do not treat this as proof of ownership of Jean Anesto's original game.
- Repository decision: hold implementation until this project has an applicable license or written permission. No contact was made. This decision does not purport to resolve game-mechanics copyright law across jurisdictions.
- General context: the U.S. Copyright Office says game methods are not protected by copyright while sufficiently expressive rules text and graphics may be; this is not a project license or a global legal opinion: <https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html>.
