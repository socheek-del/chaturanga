# Djambi rules-source notes

The four-player source profile is an 81-square 9×9 board with a central Labyrinth and four corner armies. Each army has a Chief, Assassin, Reporter, Necromobile, Diplomat/Provocateur, and four Militants. Board Game Arena documents an ordinary red-blue-yellow-green cycle; when a Chief holds the Labyrinth, its side receives additional turns after opponents. Most pieces move along unobstructed queen lines; Militants have a two-square limit. Dead pieces remain as corpses. The special pieces move living enemies, move corpses, kill at a destination or adjacent to a Reporter, and place corpses differently.

The sources note interpretive choices, including friendly-piece capture, optional Reporter kills, corpse-surrounded Chiefs, and special attacks involving the Labyrinth. These need a source comparison and a pinned contract before implementation if rights are cleared. No rulebook prose or board artwork is copied here.

Sources: <https://fr.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=djambi>, <https://www.regledujeu.fr/djambi/>.
