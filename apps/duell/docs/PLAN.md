# Duell

## Selected edition

The roadmap target is the **1976 Lakeside 8×9 Duell**, from Geoffrey Hayes's Conquest/Duell game family.
It is a distinct edition from the later 9×9 Tactix reissue. The evidence supports calling the Lakeside
edition well documented; it does not establish that it is the most popular. See the cited report and source
notes under `docs/`.

The surviving 1976 rules scan describes a 9×8 array with a 5-1-2-6-K-6-2-1-5 back rank, 3s facing the
players, orthogonal die tumbling with at most one right-angle turn, no passing over pieces, exact-landing
captures, and King/key-square victory. A rules engine would need to persist every die's orientation as part of
the game state.

## Rights gate

Duell is blocked before implementation. No digital adaptation permission or applicable license was found,
and present-day rights ownership/chain of title is unresolved. An expired U.S. patent does not by itself
establish rights clearance for a faithful digital adaptation or for expressive materials and branding. Obtain
documented rights clearance or establish an applicable license before implementation. Clear the name before
public branding. No rights-holder contact was sent.

## Open rules questions

- The scanned rules and later transcriptions conflict about whether White, Red, or a player-chosen side starts.
  Verify the original Lakeside leaflet.
- Verify die chirality/orientation against the original setup diagram.
- The source is unclear whether reaching the key square wins immediately or only after surviving a reply.
- No-move, repetition and draw outcomes are unspecified.

Do not resolve these or begin engine work until the rights gate clears.
