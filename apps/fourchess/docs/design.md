# Fourfold Chess — Wayfinder

## Direction

Treat the four-seat board like a tactical navigation chart: an open, light field, a strong cross shape, and four clear player colors entering from the compass points. The interface should feel deliberate and legible while the game has many pieces and a changing turn order.

## Identity

- Product name: **Fourfold Chess**. Describe the rules as Four-handed chess, Free-for-All.
- Use an original compass-cross mark made from four tapered arms around a small center square.
- Red, blue, ochre and green identify the four seats. Keep piece tokens visually distinct from the board with a dark ink outline and a short piece glyph.
- Use a warm paper field, deep pine ink, and a fine chart-line border. Reserve saturated colors for player identity, checks and turn markers.

## Type and interaction

- Use Literata for display headings and lesson titles, IBM Plex Sans for controls, coordinates and move records. Fonts are self-hosted with their SIL Open Font License notices.
- Keep the 14×14 cross centered and square. On narrow screens, move player cards and move history below the board without horizontal scrolling.
- Show the active seat and points beside the board; always keep all four seat scores visible.
- Use reduced-motion-safe focus and selection states. The legal-move markers, selected square and last move must remain distinguishable without color alone.

## Do not use

- No Chess.com naming, layouts, icons, diagrams, logos, art or copied rule prose.
- No chat, implied alliances, or communication controls in FFA rooms.
- No domain text in board or promotional screenshots.
