# Game of the Generals plan

## Status

Blocked before implementation while digital-adaptation rights and the current title chain are unresolved.
No rights holder or distributor has been contacted.

## Selected edition

Use the original two-player Salpakan / Game of the Generals rules with a human referee replaced by a
server-side arbiter. Its creator's first-person history dates conception to 1967 and public introduction to
1972. Available sources identify it as a distinct, still-published product; no evidence supports choosing a
popularity winner among the physical set editions.

## Rights gate

The reviewed sources identify the creator and an official global distributor, and one current distributor
page says Pasola held the patent. A modern independent browser adaptation also disclaims official
endorsement. No project-specific digital license or written permission was found, and the present chain of
title was not verified. The repository's gate blocks a faithful product until applicable permission or a
license is documented. Do not contact anyone without owner authorization.

See [`reports/Game of the Generals rules adoption and rights.md`](reports/Game%20of%20the%20Generals%20rules%20adoption%20and%20rights.md).
