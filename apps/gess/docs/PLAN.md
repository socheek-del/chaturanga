# Gess

## Candidate ruleset

The 1994 Archimedeans rules reproduced in *Eureka* 53 are the clearest surviving baseline for this entry.
The available adoption evidence does not establish that it is the most popular version. The research report
and notes are under `docs/reports/` and `docs/research_notes/`.

The candidate game uses an 18×18 board, 43 stones per player, six single-stone pawns and six back-row
formations. Players slide any eligible 3×3 own-color footprint; the outer stones enable directions, and the
center controls movement range. First collision captures every overlapped stone, including friendly ones.
Players win by preserving a ring pattern. See the report for the complete account and source conflicts.

## Release gate

Gess is blocked before implementation. No applicable license or written permission for a digital adaptation
was found. The 1995 repost source grants text repost permission only; it does not authorize a digital game.
Confirm the current rights holder and obtain written permission for a free GPL-3.0 adaptation, or establish an
applicable license, before engine or product work. No rights-holder contact has been sent. Independently
author any eventual rules text, lesson examples, artwork and interface, and clear product branding before
public use.

Additional rules work before engine design: transcribe the exact setup from an authoritative diagram; resolve
the conflict over whether a valid footprint needs an outer stone; choose explicit no-legal-move and repetition
outcomes. Do this only after the rights gate clears.
