# Hexagonal chess

## Candidate ruleset

Use Gliński's 91-cell hexagonal chess as the roadmap default and best-known form. Historical sources describe
its broad adoption compared with other hexagonal chess proposals, but no current representative popularity
ranking is available. McCooey uses a related board but is a distinct ruleset and should not be mixed into the
selected game. See the research report and notes under `docs/`.

The candidate is played on the 91-cell hexagonal board. Pritchard and Beasley's full rules support the pawn
double-step, no castling, and special stalemate scoring rather than a draw. En passant appears in some
detailed secondary rules but needs confirmation against the original book; modern summaries conflict. Before
implementation, verify the original setup diagram cell by cell and resolve en passant and the full draw rules.

## Rights gate

The feature is blocked before implementation. No applicable digital-adaptation permission or license was
found, and the present rights holder/successor for Gliński's 1973 publication is unverified. The fact that
rules descriptions are online does not grant adaptation rights. Obtain applicable licensing/permission and
clear the product name before any public branding. No contact was sent.
