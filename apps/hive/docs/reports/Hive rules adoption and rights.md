# Hive rules adoption and rights

**Review date:** 2026-11-26
**Decision:** Block implementation until an applicable digital-adaptation license or written permission is documented. No contact has been made.

## Selected planning edition

Use the standard two-player base Hive game described by Gen42's official rules PDF. The PDF says each player starts with 11 pieces and describes the central rules: surround the opposing Queen Bee, place tiles while preserving the one-hive connection, move pieces according to their creature, and place the Queen by the fourth turn. The publisher's Hive Ultimate product contains all expansions, but the roadmap's default is the base game. The available sources establish this as the canonical product profile, not as a statistically proven most-popular edition.

## Rights evidence

Gen42's official rules PDF is marked © 2010 Gen42 Games and names John Yianni as author. Gen42's About Us page identifies him as the inventor and says digital games developers should contact the company. The official pages reviewed did not provide a general public license for an independent digital adaptation. The Gen42 website terms reserve rights in the site's contents and require prior written permission to reuse those contents; this is recorded as a website-content restriction, not as a definitive statement about the legal status of abstract game rules.

Because this is a publisher-created game and the repo requires a cleared basis before scheduling digital implementation, the digital adaptation remains blocked. The user's broad goal to digitize applicable games does not authorize sending a message to Gen42. Preserve original assets and prose; do not begin code until permission or a suitable license is documented.
