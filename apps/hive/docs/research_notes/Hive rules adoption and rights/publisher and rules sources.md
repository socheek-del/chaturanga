# Publisher and rules sources

Reviewed 2026-11-26. Facts below are paraphrased from official Gen42 sources.

## Rules PDF

URL: https://www.gen42.com/wp-content/uploads/Hive-rules.pdf

- The first page states © 2010 Gen42 Games and names John Yianni as author.
- Each player has 11 pieces in the base game.
- The object is to surround the opposing Queen Bee.
- Pieces build a boardless hive; moves must preserve a connected hive.
- The Queen must be placed by the player's fourth turn.
- Expansion pieces have separate rules sections and are not part of the base 11-piece inventory.

These facts support the rules profile only. The PDF's content and artwork are not reused.

## Gen42 About Us

URL: https://www.gen42.com/about-us/

- Gen42 says John Yianni invented Hive.
- The page says digital games developers should get in touch with Gen42.

Inference: the publisher invites a licensing/permission inquiry, but the page does not itself grant one.

## Gen42 Hive Ultimate product page

URL: https://www.gen42.com/product/hive-ultimate/

- Hive Ultimate is described as the same base game plus the Ladybug, Mosquito and Pillbug expansions.
- The listed player count is two.
- The official shop sells the physical product.

This product listing does not establish which expansion profile is most used. The project retains the roadmap's base-game default without a popularity claim.
