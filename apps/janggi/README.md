# 장기 · Janggi

Janggi, Korean chess, free in the browser: six computer opponents, two players on one device, online rooms by
code or quick match, and 14 lessons. Korean and English, no account, no ads, works offline.

한국어: [README.ko.md](README.ko.md) · Rules as implemented: [packages/janggi/RULES.md](../../packages/janggi/RULES.md) ·
For developers: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)

- Cannons must jump to move; chariots, cannons and soldiers use the palace diagonals.
- Choose one of four opening arrangements, pass your turn, call bikjang; the points decide undecided games.
- The engine agrees with Fairy-Stockfish move for move.
