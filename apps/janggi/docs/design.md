# "Dancheong" (단청): design proposal, awaiting owner approval

The painted eaves of Korean palaces: indigo `#1f4e8c` (primary), jade `#0f6b4f` (secondary, and Cho's ink),
vermilion `#b3261e` (danger, and Han's ink) and ochre, on a cool paper canvas, with a five-colour band on hero
cards. The mark is an octagon, the shape of a Janggi piece.

Boards: Ginkgo (은행나무, default), Hanji (한지), Night (밤). Pieces are octagons in three sizes. The showcase is
at `/design`; tokens are in `apps/janggi/web/src/index.css`.
