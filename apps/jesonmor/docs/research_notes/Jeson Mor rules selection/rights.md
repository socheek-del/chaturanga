# Rights and permissions

## Copyright and traditional-culture scope

### Takeaway

The available official sources separate game methods from expressive rule text, software and art, but the treatment of folklore depends on jurisdiction. This review does not establish worldwide permission to publish a Jeson Mor adaptation.

### Cited Findings

- The U.S. Copyright Office says a game's idea, title and methods of play are not protected by U.S. copyright; it says rule text and graphic art can be copyrightable expression. [Copyright Office: Games](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html)
- Mongolia's 2021 revised copyright law, in its WIPO Lex English translation, excludes works of folklore and national traditions from copyright protection under that law and excludes ideas/methods; it separately lists derivative works based on folklore among protected works, and Article 3.2 directs folk-art protection to other relevant laws. [WIPO Lex: Law on Copyright and Related Rights](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/8306)
- The WIPO overview says traditional cultural expressions may be protected by several existing IP systems, some countries have special folklore laws, and WIPO's intergovernmental committee is negotiating international protection. [WIPO: Traditional cultural expressions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/traditional-cultural-expressions/index)
- Mongolia's Constitution states that historical, cultural, scientific and intellectual heritage of the Mongolian people is under state protection. [WIPO Lex: Constitution of Mongolia](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/3039)

### Inferences

- A factual implementation of game procedures, written independently, is a lower-risk basis than copying an author's prose, diagram, code or visual design. The U.S. rule is jurisdiction-specific, and Mongolia's folklore provisions plus its reference to other laws prevent treating “traditional” as a worldwide permission grant.
- The game should be described respectfully as a Mongolian game, without implying official Mongolian endorsement or ownership clearance based on these materials.

### Gaps

- No local Mongolian legal opinion was obtained on whether this particular game or its current forms qualify as protected folk art under other relevant laws.
- No comprehensive patent, trademark, unfair-competition or country-by-country clearance was performed.

## Rule text, board art and existing implementations

### Takeaway

The consulted sources identify historical rule references and several digital implementations, but no open license or permission to reuse their expressive materials was found. Their factual descriptions can guide independently written rules; their text, art and code should not be copied without checking the specific license or permission.

### Cited Findings

- The Jeson Mor article cites David B. Pritchard's *The Encyclopedia of Chess Variants* (1994) and *The Classified Encyclopedia of Chess Variants* (2007) as its references. The article does not identify an open license for those books' prose or diagrams. [Jeson Mor bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeson_Mor)
- An online Jeson Mor page describes an original in-browser implementation and its own rule explanation; no code/art reuse license is stated on the game page reviewed. [VariChess: Jeson Mor](https://varichess.org/play/jesonmor)
- An itch.io listing identifies a Windows/Linux Jeson Mor prototype by “hidroto” and offers a download/name-your-price purchase; the listing reviewed does not state a source-code or asset license. A download offer alone does not state permission to reuse its code or art. [Jeson Mor by hidroto](https://hidroto.itch.io/jeson-mor)
- Board Game Arena documents that its platform seeks authorization for online adaptations of non-public-domain titles. This is BGA's own licensing policy; it does not establish a Jeson Mor rightsholder or a permission requirement for unrelated platforms. [Board Game Arena game licences](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/BGA_Game_licences)
- Wikimedia says most Wikipedia article text uses CC BY-SA 4.0, while individual media items can carry different licenses and need their own license check. [Wikimedia content reuse](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_APIs/Content_reuse)

### Inferences

- Use Pritchard's citations and other sources to identify rules facts, then write an original rules contract, tutorial and UI copy. Do not reproduce the encyclopedia text, the Wikimedia setup diagram, a digital site's prose, or another implementation's assets/code unless the precise file/page license grants the planned use and its terms are followed.
- Draw original horse-piece artwork and board/brand assets. If any Wikipedia/Wikimedia image is considered later, check the individual Commons file page and comply with its own attribution/license rather than relying on the article-text license.

### Gaps

- The consulted rules references do not disclose a current publisher or licensor for the underlying traditional game, and no direct permission policy or contact for a game rights holder was located.
- The source code and assets of the inspected online and downloadable implementations were not licensed for this project in the pages reviewed; their complete terms, if any, were not independently audited.
- No trademark database clearance for “Jeson Mor” or its transliterations was completed; copyright and trademark questions are separate.

## Recommended product boundary

### Takeaway

Proceed only with an independently authored digital implementation, while keeping the source and cultural-heritage limitations visible in the product notes. Do not rely on an assumed public-domain status for all expressions associated with the game.

### Cited Findings

- U.S. Copyright Office guidance distinguishes uncopyrightable game methods from potentially protected written rules and board/container artwork. [Copyright Office: Games](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html)
- Mongolia's current law translation distinguishes folklore/national traditions and ideas/methods from protected creative works while also covering folklore-based derivative works and referring folk-art protection to other relevant laws. [WIPO Lex: Law on Copyright and Related Rights](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/8306)
- WIPO identifies traditional cultural expressions as a distinct IP-policy subject and notes that protection can involve multiple legal systems. [WIPO: Traditional cultural expressions](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/traditional-cultural-expressions/index)

### Inferences

- For this repository, the source-bounded implementation boundary is original rules prose, code, piece art, tutorial content, visual identity and sound; cite sources used to verify factual rules, but do not imply source text or images are licensed for reuse.
- Keep the product branding clear of any other implementation's logo, trade dress or screenshots; treat “Jeson Mor” as a game-name reference pending a separate trademark review.

### Gaps

- This review is a research note for implementation planning, not a global legal clearance. Territory-specific advice remains unresolved if a later launch strategy or rights-holder evidence makes it necessary.
