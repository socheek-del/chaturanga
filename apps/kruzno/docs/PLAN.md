# Kruzno

## Rules identity

The catalog and its linked material describe two related but distinct games. Wikipedia's main Kruzno entry is
Mark Palko's commercial 2005 base game, with nine pieces per side and a special cyclic capture relation. The
official site separately documents **Kruzno Checkers**, a 12-piece checkers variant on the Kruzno board.
Because the roadmap explicitly calls for the linked hex-checkers setup and jumps, the candidate for this
product is Kruzno Checkers; do not silently mix it with the base game's pieces or capture rules.

Available use evidence is local and sparse (including a public village board and a few sets), so it does not
show which variant is most popular. The rules notes also flag incomplete setup/capture details and unavailable
diagrams that would need confirmation before a Variant contract.

## Rights gate

Kruzno is blocked before implementation. No digital adaptation license or permission was found, and the
present copyright/title chain is unresolved. A secondary report of the U.S. KRUZNO trademark cancellation in
2022 does not provide worldwide or common-law name clearance. Obtain applicable rights clearance before
coding, and review the public name separately. No contact was sent.
