# Martian Chess rules adoption and rights

**Research date:** 2026-11-26  
**Decision:** Block implementation pending written permission from Looney Labs. No contact has been made.

## Selected planning edition

The roadmap selects the current two-player base game. Looney Labs describes ownership as determined by a piece's location on the board and lists Martian Chess in Pyramid Arcade. The publisher identifies Andrew Looney as designer, 1995 as the creation year, and 1996 as the first publication. No source reviewed provides participation statistics comparing the two-player form, larger player-count variants, or equipment combinations, so no popularity winner is claimed.

## Rights evidence

Looney Labs' official policy for electronic versions says a hobby programmer seeking to publish a freeware electronic adaptation should contact the company and request permission before anything is made available online. The policy describes additional conditions: the adaptation must faithfully represent the original and include promotion for the physical product. Looney Labs lists existing online Martian Chess adaptations, but that does not grant permission to this project.

The repository's rights gate requires permission before implementation. The owner has not authorized external contact, and no contact has been made. Do not copy official rules wording, pyramid art, logos, diagrams, or product identity while blocked.
