# Ploy

## Selected rules candidate

Use the original 1970 Frank Thibault / 3M rules as the best-documented candidate. The two-player game is
the recommended default because the contemporary *Abstract Games* review describes it as the main form;
available evidence does not establish it as the most popular. The 3M manual also documents authentic
four-player individual and partnership modes, making multiplayer applicable if rights clear. The later
1982 Schmidt *Imperium* republication is related, but any differences need verification against the 3M rules.

The original manual describes a 9×9-point board, 15 directional pieces per player, directional movement and
capture by landing. A player wins by capturing the opposing Commander or all other opposing pieces. Some
pieces can rotate; the manual scan leaves the exact rotation increment unclear, while later sources conflict.
Seat/color mapping and draw/no-move outcomes also need resolution before implementation.

## Rights gate

Ploy is blocked before implementation. It is attributed to Frank Thibault and was published by 3M, but the
current rights holder and Ploy-specific chain of title could not be established. No applicable license or
digital adaptation permission was found. Hasbro's general digital games policy does not establish that Hasbro
owns Ploy. Obtain documented ownership and permission or an applicable license before coding, and clear the
title/branding before public release. No rights-holder contact was sent.
