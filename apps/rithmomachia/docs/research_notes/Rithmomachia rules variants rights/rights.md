# Rithmomachia rights research

Research checked 2026-11-26. This is a scoped source review, not legal advice.

## Findings

- The selected rules are historical game mechanics summarized in the linked Wikipedia article and
  compared against historical sources. U.S. Copyright Office guidance says game methods are not
  protected by copyright, while original prose, diagrams, and artwork may be protected.
- The 1616/17 Selenus book is a primary historical witness, cataloged by the Bayerische
  Staatsbibliothek. Its digitized object is marked **No Copyright—Non-Commercial Use Only**. Treat
  that as a restriction on the scan image: do not ship the image or the library's modern translation.
- The Mebben/du CŒur page does not display an adaptation license. Ann Moyer's 2001 illustrated
  modern edition reserves its rights. The 2021 academic proposal is published under CC BY-NC-ND
  4.0. Do not copy their text, tables, illustrations, translations, or specific editorial choices.
- The historical game name and basic board/piece mechanics are independently implemented in new
  code. The tutorial, diagrams, art, and UI copy will be original. Historical information is used as
  research, not reproduced as expressive material.

## Assessment

The repository can proceed with an independently authored implementation of historical rules
mechanics. This does not establish worldwide clearance, and the game's product name still needs a
separate brand review before public promotion. No rights-holder contact has been made.
