# Rollerball chess

## Selected game

The roadmap refers to Jean-Louis Cazaux's 40-square Rollerball chess variant, dated 1998 by Pritchard and
1999 by later sources. It is unrelated to the 1975 Invicta Rollerball tilt-maze game. The evidence identifies
one ruleset but does not establish comparative popularity. See the report and source notes under `docs/`.

The candidate uses a two-ring 40-square topology with six-piece armies and location-dependent moves. Pieces
can rebound from the board edge; pawns do not double-step. Promotion, checkmate, clockwise King-race wins,
stalemate and threefold repetition appear in the available transcription. Verify the exact start diagram and
what “travelled clockwise” means against Cazaux's original rules before encoding it.

## Rights gate

Rollerball is blocked before implementation. No applicable digital-adaptation license or permission was
found. Cazaux's original rules page could not be inspected during research, and the evidence does not establish
a license covering a faithful digital product. Obtain applicable rights clearance before implementation;
review title/brand separately before public release. No contact was sent.
