# Senterej design direction — Field of Ochre

**Status: proposed; owner review pending.**

The product is a quiet digital strategy desk, shaped around the historical uncheckered red playing surface.
The main board uses one parchment tone with fine oxide lines, so it reads as an open field rather than a modern
checkerboard. Ivory and Gold pieces use original geometric marks, drawn for this app rather than copied from a
historic set.

The interface pairs a bookish serif for titles with a compact, clear sans serif for controls and move records.
Ink, warm limestone and a restrained red-orange carry most of the interface; a small mineral blue marks
selected actions. Page composition borrows from a field notebook: wide margins, numbered lesson cards,
annotated diagrams and a narrow move ledger. It does not imitate a manuscript, a particular ethnic textile or
the visual language of another game product.

Motion is reserved for move arrival, legal-target focus and tutorial feedback. Every board action remains
available by tap, and all board cells keep spoken labels and visible coordinates.
