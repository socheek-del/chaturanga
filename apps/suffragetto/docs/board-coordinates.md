# Suffragetto board coordinates

This is a coordinate transcription of the surviving Sargeant Bros. second-edition board and its marked
setup cells. It records geometry only; it does not reproduce the archival image, typography, colours, or
rules text.

## Coordinate frame

- The board has 17 files (`a` through `q`) and 17 ranks (`1` through `17`).
- `a1` is the bottom-left corner from the Suffragette side. Rank 1 is the Suffragette edge; rank 17 is the
  Police edge. Files increase left to right.
- Every one of the 289 coordinates is a board cell. Holding and staging cells are not ordinary routes or
  destinations; they are reserved for captures and exchange releases.
- The central Arena envelope is files `e`–`m`, ranks `1`–`17` (9×17). The two six-cell buildings are cut
  out of the pink Arena: goal cells are not Arena capture squares.

## Named areas

| Area | Coordinate mask | Count | Use |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Pink Arena | files `e`–`m`, ranks `1`–`17`, excluding both buildings | 141 | Pieces here can be captured by a qualifying hop. |
| House of Commons | files `h`–`j`, ranks `13`–`14` | 6 | Suffragette goal. Police cannot enter; Suffragettes cannot leave after entering. |
| Albert Hall | files `h`–`j`, ranks `4`–`5` | 6 | Police goal. Suffragettes cannot enter; Police cannot leave after entering. |
| Prison | file `a`, ranks `1`–`17`, plus file `b`, ranks `14`–`17` | 21 | Captured Suffragettes are held here. |
| Prison Yard | file `b`, ranks `1`–`6` | 6 | Suffragettes released by an exchange start here. |
| Hospital | file `q`, ranks `1`–`17`, plus file `p`, ranks `1`–`4` | 21 | Disabled Police are held here. |
| Hospital Grounds | file `p`, ranks `12`–`17` | 6 | Police released by an exchange start here. |

All other cells, including the seven middle cells of files `b` and `p`, are ordinary board cells. A piece
may not land in or hop across a Prison, Prison Yard, Hospital, or Hospital Grounds cell. Capture and
exchange transfers are the only ways to place a piece in a holding or staging area.

## Marked setup cells

The marked `S` and `P` cells are symmetric across the board's middle rank. There are 21 per side:

- `S`: `{g4, k4}`, `{e5, f5, g5, k5, l5, m5}`, and every file `c`–`o` on rank 6.
- `P`: every file `c`–`o` on rank 12, `{e13, f13, g13, k13, l13, m13}`, and `{g14, k14}`.

The five large Suffragette pieces start at `i6`, `c6`, `o6`, `e5`, and `m5`; the five large Police pieces
start at `i12`, `c12`, `o12`, `e13`, and `m13`. The central front-row pieces are the Leader and Inspector;
the other four large pieces occupy the ends of the front and second rows. The other 16 marked cells per
side hold rank-and-file pieces.

## Counts for fixture verification

- Board cells: `17 × 17 = 289`.
- Arena envelope: `9 × 17 = 153`; the two buildings occupy 12 cells, leaving 141 pink Arena cells.
- Prison and Hospital: 21 cells each; Prison Yard and Hospital Grounds: 6 cells each.
- Remaining ordinary cells outside the reserved holding/staging areas: `289 - 2×21 - 2×6 = 235`.
- Starting marked cells: 21 Suffragettes and 21 Police; five large and 16 rank-and-file per side.

## Evidence

The exact masks were transcribed from the marked board and `S`/`P` positions in the Games Board Association
catalogue photographs of the surviving second edition. The printed rules sheet identifies which pieces
start at those marks and defines the goal buildings, Arena and holding areas. The archival reference was
used for factual geometry only; all shipped diagrams, board styling, piece art and copy will be original.
