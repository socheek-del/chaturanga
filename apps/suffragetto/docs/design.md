# Suffragetto design: The field guide

## Direction

Present Suffragetto as a playable field guide to a historical board game: archival in its materials, clear in its modern controls, and original in its artwork. Use the 17×17 layout as the product's visual signature.

## Materials and colour

- Warm paper (`#eee9df`) behind cream cards (`#fbf8f0`), with thin rules and quiet shadows.
- Campaign green (`#294d40`) marks the Suffragettes and primary actions.
- Slate blue (`#334b62`) marks the Police.
- Dusty rose (`#ce9695`) identifies the pink Arena; a muted ochre marks the House, and blue-grey marks Albert Hall.
- Use Georgia for editorial headings and Noto Sans for controls, board labels and body text.

## Board and pieces

Keep all 17 ranks and files visible, with readable coordinates. The board underlay shows the Arena, both buildings, Prison, Hospital and staging yards. All zones have shape and label cues as well as colour. Small pieces are stamped discs; large officers use a rosette silhouette. White and black colours follow the rules engine's seat names, while visible labels identify the Suffragettes and Police.

## Interaction

The board is the main working surface. A route is selected and completed as one move; an exchange is offered as an explicit action when legal. Tutorial prompts, hints, legal routes and feedback stay close to the board. On phones, keep the board and important actions in view and let secondary notes flow below.

## Product boundaries

The home screen, online lobby, lessons, PWA assets and all text belong to this product. Use original SVG artwork and product-written rules summaries. Do not show a website address inside screenshots or share images.
