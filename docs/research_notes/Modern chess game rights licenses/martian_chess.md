# Martian Chess

## Does current primary-source evidence clear the digital-adaptation gate?

### Takeaway
No. Looney Labs' official electronic-version policy still directs hobby programmers to ask for permission before publishing a freeware electronic version, and no public license or permission for this project appears on its official Martian Chess or policy pages. The repository's pre-implementation gate therefore remains closed unless the owner authorizes a request and Looney Labs grants permission.

### Cited Findings
- The official Electronic Arcade page, crawled on 2026-11-27, labels its policy “Updated July 2019.” It says fan-built freeware adaptations should be submitted for permission before anything is made available online, and that permitted freeware should faithfully adapt the game and promote the physical product. [Looney Labs Electronic Arcade](https://www.looneylabs.com/looney-labs-electronic-arcade)
- The same official page lists existing Martian Chess adaptations on Board Game Arena and Abstract Play. Those listings describe other implementations and do not grant this project permission. [Looney Labs Electronic Arcade](https://www.looneylabs.com/looney-labs-electronic-arcade)
- Looney Labs' current product page lists Martian Chess as a two-player product and describes its current game. It provides no public digital-adaptation license. [Martian Chess product page](https://www.looneylabs.com/games/martian-chess)
- The publisher's Contact Us page links to “Can I make an Electronic Version?” and lists an email for other inquiries; the linked guidance returns to the permission-first Electronic Arcade policy. [Looney Labs Contact Us](https://www.looneylabs.com/contact-us)

### Inferences
- Because the publisher's published path requires permission before an online freeware adaptation, existing digital versions are not evidence of a general license. Under the repository rule, implementation should not start absent project-specific permission or an applicable license.
- No contact or license request was made in this review.

### Gaps
- No public license covering this project's faithful online adaptation was found in the official pages checked.
- The policy is explicitly dated July 2019; whether Looney Labs would grant this project permission, and what conditions it would impose, remains unknown until the owner authorizes a request and the publisher responds.
