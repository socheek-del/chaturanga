# Chaturanga rules

This product selects the two-player reconstruction attributed to al-Adli in the Digital Ludeme Project. The source differs from other reconstructions in its placement, elephant move and stalemate outcome. A reliable comparison of the historical reconstructions' popularity is unavailable.

- The game uses an 8×8 board. On White’s back rank the order is elephant, chariot, horse, adviser, king, horse, chariot, elephant. Black uses the same piece groups with king and adviser on opposite files. Eight infantry start immediately in front of each back rank. White moves first.
- The gaja jumps two squares along a rank or file, over any intervening piece. The ratha slides along ranks and files. The ashva jumps like a modern knight. The mantri steps one square diagonally, and the raja steps one square in any direction.
- Infantry move one square straight forward, capture one square diagonally forward and promote to mantri on the far rank. Castling, double infantry moves and en passant are absent.
- A move must leave the mover’s king safe. Checkmate wins for the other side. Stalemate wins for the player who has no legal move. Removing all opposing pieces except the king wins immediately.
- The digital edition draws on a third repetition or after 70 moves by each side without a capture or infantry move. These are digital conventions for prolonged games.

FEN uses `b` for gaja and `q` for mantri. A promoted move uses the `q` suffix. The board setup follows the published Ludii model. The promotion rule follows the description of infantry in the Wikipedia account.

Sources: [Digital Ludeme Project reconstruction](https://ludii.games/details.php?keyword=Chaturanga+%28al-Adli%29), [Original Ludii rules model](https://ludii.games/lud/games/Chaturanga%20%28al-Adli%29.lud), [Chaturanga overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaturanga).
