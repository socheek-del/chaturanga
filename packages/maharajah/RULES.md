# Maharajah and the Sepoys

White starts with the royal Maharajah on e1. Black starts with the usual chess army. White moves first. The Maharajah combines queen slides and knight leaps. It can slide across attacked squares, but its landing square must be safe. Both sides win by checkmate.

Black pawns advance toward rank one and can advance two squares from rank seven if both squares are clear. They capture diagonally and remain immobile pawns on rank one. This edition has no castling or en passant. Stalemate, threefold repetition and 100 halfmoves without a pawn move or capture are draws.

The rules follow the [Wikipedia game rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maharajah_and_the_Sepoys#Game_rules) and the [documented no-castling rules](https://sites.google.com/site/boardandpieces/list-of-games/maharajah-and-the-sepoys). Automatic repetition and halfmove draws are digital conventions. The rules suite compares seeded positions with Fairy-Stockfish configured with a royal amazon, no castling and no promotion region.

FEN uses uppercase M for the white Maharajah and the standard lowercase chess letters for the black army. Castling and en passant fields are always hyphens. Moves use standard four-character UCI coordinates.
